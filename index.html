The problem

Students leave mid-lease. The rent doesn't.

Summer internships, study abroad and co-ops pull students away while their lease keeps running. Finding someone to take the room is left to whatever group chat or post you can find.

Empty rooms, full rent

Students keep paying for a room they aren't living in, sometimes for months.

Scattered, untrusted channels

Sublets get passed around Facebook groups, Reddit threads and department chats. They're hard to search and hard to trust.

Scams hit hardest where it hurts

Unverified posts make it easy to get scammed, and international students are often the ones targeted.

How it works

Three ways in, one good match.

Sublette is built around the match. Every part of the app works toward pairing a room with the student who fits it best.

MAP

See rooms where you'll be

Browse available sublets on a map around campus, so you can judge the walk to class or the commute to your internship at a glance.

MATCHMAKER

AI ranks the best fits

In the background, a matchmaker weighs dates, budget, location and preferences on both sides, and puts the strongest matches first.

SWIPE FEED

Swipe to match

Swipe through your ranked feed. When a lister and a renter both say yes, it's a match and you can start talking.

Pricing

Plan ahead, pay less.

Listers pay per listing, priced as a small share of that room's rent. The further ahead you list, the cheaper it is. Each additional listing is priced on its own.

We're setting the exact rates through interviews with students before launch.
